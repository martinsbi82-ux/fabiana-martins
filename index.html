About

I'm a Commercial Operations Consultant working at the intersection of sales, process design, and technology.

01 — Who I am

Structured operator, not a theorist

I work with B2B and SaaS companies across Brazil and abroad, building the commercial infrastructure that sits underneath growth — pipelines, reporting, documentation, and the tools that make them run.

02 — The problem I solve

Commercial operations built on tribal knowledge

Most growing companies run their sales engine on habits nobody wrote down: pipelines live in one person's head, CRMs are half-used, and reports are rebuilt from scratch every month. I replace that with systems the whole team can rely on.

03 — My differentiator

Hands-on experience, not slide decks

My background blends direct commercial representation experience with process design and AI tooling, so what I build is working infrastructure people actually use — not a framework that stays in a proposal.

How I Work

A five-stage delivery framework

Every engagement follows the same sequence — deliberately, so nothing gets built on assumptions.

Stage 01

Discovery

Interviews, shadowing, and a review of existing tools to understand how commercial work actually happens today.

Stage 02

Assessment

Mapping gaps between the current process and what the business needs to scale — with clear priorities.

Stage 03

Strategy

Designing the target operating model — process, CRM structure, and reporting — and getting stakeholder buy-in.

Stage 04

Implementation

Building the systems, documenting the playbooks, and training the team to run the new process.

Stage 05

Continuous Improvement

Reviewing what's working with real usage data, and iterating the system as the business grows.
