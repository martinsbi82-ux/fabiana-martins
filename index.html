The diagnosis

Sales teams that work harder and sell at the same pace have an operations problem, not an effort problem.

An underused CRM, a pipeline with no criteria, targets with no route, commissions with no logic: different symptoms of the same cause — the commercial operation was never designed, it just kept growing.

Symptom 01

Opaque pipeline

No one knows, with precision, what stage each opportunity is in — or why it advances or stalls.

Symptom 02

Targets disconnected from coverage

Targets are set by spreadsheet, not by the real capacity to cover territory and accounts.

Symptom 03

Commissions with no commercial logic

The incentive plan rewards volume, not the behavior the commercial strategy actually requires.

The method

Commercial Reset Framework™: four phases, one redesigned operation.
